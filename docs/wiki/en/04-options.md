# Options

The mod has one option, set by each player in **Options > Mods > Eris Food Expiry (batman fork)**. It adds no sandbox option of its own.

## Exact times need Nutritionist

Off by default.

- **Off**: everyone sees the exact times.
- **On**: exact times are shown when the character has the Nutritionist trait (trait or profession), or when the food is packaged and its label is readable, like the vanilla nutrition facts: the character is not illiterate, there is enough light to read, and the label is present. Otherwise the tooltip shows a rough state (see [Reading the labels](02-reading-the-labels.md#rough-state)).

> Capture à faire : Options > Mods with the "Exact times need Nutritionist" tick box.

## Vanilla sandbox options that change the times

| Sandbox option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Food Spoilage | Overall spoilage speed. |
| Refrigeration Effectiveness | Slowdown in a powered fridge or freezer (down to no decay). |
| Electricity Shutoff | Last day of grid power for fridges and freezers. |
| Rotten Food Removal | Shows **Disappears in** on rotten food when on. |

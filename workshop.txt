description=[h1]Eris Food Expiry (TooltipLib fork)[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Unofficial fork · Build 42.21 · Singleplayer and multiplayer[/b]
description=
description=How long before this milk turns? Hover any food: the tooltip shows its freshness and the exact time left before it goes stale and rots. Expand a stack in your inventory to see a thin freshness strip under each item.
description=
description=[h2]Two original mods, one fork[/h2]
description=
description=This mod continues the work of two Workshop items. Thanks to their authors.
description=[list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3392259028][B42] eris food expiry[/url]: Build 42 port of eris's food expiry mod, with the Nutritionist option.
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3629527156][B42.13] eris food expiry[/url]: update for 42.13 singleplayer, exact times without the trait.
description=[/list]
description=Both use the mod ID [i]eris_food_expiry[/i]: this fork is marked incompatible with it. Enable only one of them.
description=
description=[h2]What you see[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Freshness[/b] bar: full when the food is new, empty when it is rotten.
description=[*][b]Stale in[/b] and [b]Rotten in[/b]: time left, largest units first (years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes).
description=[*]Frozen food: "Frozen: not spoiling". Canned and dry food: "Does not expire".
description=[*]Option (Options > Mods): require the Nutritionist trait for exact times. A readable package always shows them, like the vanilla nutrition facts. Otherwise you get a rough state: very fresh, fresh, looks okay, starting to rot, almost rotten, rotten.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/ErisFoodExpiry/main/docs/steam/02-fridge.jpg[/img]
description=
description=[h2]Times that follow the game[/h2]
description=
description=The time left is computed the way the game ages food:
description=[list]
description=[*]sandbox [i]Food Spoilage[/i] speed and [i]Refrigeration Effectiveness[/i];
description=[*]a fridge or freezer slows spoilage while it has power: a generator, or the grid until the shutoff day, after which the food spoils at normal speed again;
description=[*]frozen food does not spoil;
description=[*]each item's own stale and rotten ages (cooked food differs from raw food).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/ErisFoodExpiry/main/docs/steam/03-frozen.jpg[/img]
description=
description=[h2]What changed from the originals[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Tooltip lines through [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3694097672]TooltipLib[/url]: no box drawn under the tooltip, no jumping tooltip, works with other tooltip mods.
description=[*]Fridge without power, frozen food and cooked food give correct times.
description=[*]Time units always in order: the originals could show "5m 2d 1w" or drop the years.
description=[*]The "Rotten" state shows again; option texts and translations load in 42.21 (JSON).
description=[*]The inventory strip no longer overlaps the nutrition or cooking line.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cyberbobjr/ErisFoodExpiry/main/docs/steam/04-read-the-label.jpg[/img]
